# AI in Education 🤖📚

Hello! I am an educator with experience in teaching computer science and graphic design.

I currently teach computer science at Astana Technical College and also work as a graphic design instructor at the Tanym Child Development Center.

## 👩‍🏫 About Me

My professional interests include:

- Computer Science
- Graphic Design
- CorelDRAW
- Artificial Intelligence
- AI tools for education
- Digital technologies
- Creative technologies

## 🎨 Graphic Design

I teach students how to work with graphic design tools, including CorelDRAW, and help them develop practical skills in creating visual content and vector graphics.

## 🤖 Why I am interested in AI

I am interested in exploring Artificial Intelligence and Agentic AI and understanding how these technologies can be applied in education.

My goal is to improve my AI skills and learn how AI tools can help educators:

- create educational materials;
- develop creative assignments;
- automate routine tasks;
- support students;
- combine AI with graphic design;
- create more interactive learning experiences.

## 🚀 My Goal

I want to expand my knowledge of modern AI technologies, gain practical experience with AI agents and explore new ways of integrating AI into education and graphic design.

This repository will contain my experiments, ideas and projects related to AI and education.
